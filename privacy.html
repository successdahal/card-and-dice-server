Card and Dice - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Card and Dice ("the app") is a collection of card and dice games published by Success Dahal Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Play money only

All money, coins and rewards in the app are virtual play money. They have no real-world value, cannot be cashed out, and cannot be bought or sold for real money. The app does not offer gambling or real-money prizes.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or exact location. The app stores your game settings, play-money balance and progress on your own device.

For online play with friends or quick match, the app connects to our game server. The server briefly handles the player name you choose, a room code and your game moves so the game can work. This information is not used for any other purpose and is not kept after the game room closes.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show ads and measure how well they work. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings. You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google AdMob, as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
